Why it matters

Unrelieved pressure turns into a wound faster than most caregivers expect.

Clinical guidance generally calls for repositioning a bedridden patient roughly every two hours. In practice, that's hard to keep up with — especially overnight, or when one caregiver is managing everything alone.

A pressure ulcer that starts as reddened skin can become a deep wound within days, leading to pain, infection, and hospital visits that a timely reposition could have prevented.

BedSafe keeps the clock running so nobody has to remember it.

How it works

From the mattress to the caregiver's phone, in six steps.

BedSafe's hardware and software work as one continuous loop — sensing, sending, storing, and alerting without anyone in between.

01

Sense

FSR pressure sensors under the mattress read force at key body points.

02

Process

An ESP32 controller samples the sensors and identifies posture.

03

Transmit

Readings are sent over Wi-Fi as soon as they're captured.

04

Store

Firebase Realtime Database logs pressure, position, and time.

05

Display

The web dashboard shows live status and history for each patient.

06

Alert

When time or pressure crosses a safe limit, the caregiver is notified.
